## LinkedIn

### Main post — paste ready

I built a product that doesn't exist.

It's called NEON — a spatial AI companion from 2026. No screen. No edges. Technology that feels alive.

I didn't build a landing page. I built a 45-second film you can scroll.

Seven chapters. One 3D scene. The scroll is the timeline:

01 — The Arrival
02 — AI Core
03 — Intelligence
04 — Spatial UI
05 — Color Explosion
06 — The Object
07 — The Future

Built with React, Three.js, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis and custom GLSL. No stock 3D. No templates. Every object, shader and transition is generated in code.

The hardest part wasn't the 3D. It was making the scroll feel physical. Every 2–4 seconds, the whole world has to change.

I recorded a vertical cut for Reels — watch it below.

Then tell me: which chapter would you screen-record first?

### Posting notes for reach

1. **Upload the 9:16 MP4 natively.** Do not post a link as the main content; native video gets more distribution.
2. **Use a 3–5 second hook.** Put the strongest visual or the first hook line on screen immediately.
3. **Post the 16:9 cut separately** for the design/engineering audience, or as a follow-up post.
4. **Lead with a story, not a feature list.** The first two lines determine whether LinkedIn expands the post.
5. **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes.** Ask a real question in the post.
6. **Put links in the first comment.** This protects reach from external-link penalties.
7. **Tag only real, relevant accounts.** DeepSeek is relevant if you used Harness. Don't tag unrelated brands.
8. **Repurpose the two GIFs** from `assets/readme` as static/animated previews for GitHub, X and newsletters.

No one can guarantee millions of views. These choices give the work the best possible algorithmic surface: a native vertical video, a strong visual hook, a clear story and a reason to comment.
